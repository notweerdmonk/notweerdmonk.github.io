# Paper bag masquerade

Trickling tears soak,\
hearty cries they choke;\
smiles get ironed too.\
Nasty murmurs you hear,\
hostile crowd I fear;\
someone calls out, don’t know who!

This brown paper adores\
as if it self-conjures;\
I cannot feel my skin;\
I tear this brown sheath\
to feel the skin beneath,\
but there is always one within.

Sometimes I speak, everyone heeds\
though no one hears indeed.\
When I scream for vindication\
to break free of this gimmick,\
act honest and not mimic;\
this is only a grave deception.

Helpless it is as it seems,\
I pull on at the seams;\
I yearn for it to be over,\
to find the voice inside.\
The stark self that resides\
lurking beneath the paper cover.

Beware of the Devil’s game,\
quit romancing brittle fame,\
else we would bleed staining red;\
not only the fabric of mankind alone\
but also these paper faces we adorn\
and reveal the paper bag masquerade.

---
March 2011
