# Why does the supernatural exist?

_**"There are misfortunes in life that no one will accept;
people would rather believe in the supernatural and the impossible."\
                                  — Alexander Dumas, The Man in the Iron Mask**_
---

Supernatural has existed since the early humans and continues to exist; and
shall exist till the end of time. It is neither the clarity of the human mind
that eludes facts, nor the knowledge of humankind, in individual or as
a collective, that impedes the understanding of mysterious and strange
phenomena. Rather, it is the extreme confidence in perceived knowledge and
generational wisdom, imagination and the prowess of the human brain in pattern
recognition and perceptual completion. To let the unknown be as it is does not
instill the satisfaction and bestow the rewards, which a complete yet illusory 
purview of things provides. A mind at ease is _often_ but not _always_, either
ignorant or deluded, and both serve a desirable purpose. Unless under compulsion
or at least influence.

Shifting our vantage to times long ago, back to the medieval, back to the
origins of the _feudal_ society, when religion was the sole most capable
controlling instrument, it still is; when power and dominion over masses were
crucial, we begin to understand how crude human perception was. Mostly because
of the lack of information, incoherence among groups, communities and
civilizations, and above all the lack of reliance on scientific methods and
factual criticality. Hypothesizing, some king or some of their nobles, perhaps
some king along with their nobles got wary of their diminishing control over
the masses. People began questioning religion as to why the God(s) punish them
on behest of others, punish them for no wrong deeds of their own at all.
Calamities ranging from the natural ones such as famines, droughts, floods,
cyclones, hurricanes, tsunamis, tornadoes, volcanic eruptions, earthquakes,
meteor strikes, wild fires, epidemics, and others, to synthetic ones such as
pillages, conquests, wars and political conflicts; everything which overpowered
the humans could be attributed to either randomness of the world around or
deterministic and premeditated-meditated and motivated actions. The people
always sought for an explanation to complement their sorrow and despair, such
has been the perceived hierarchy among almost all emotional beings.

The king along with their nobles conferred with the clergies and the priests,
and conceived the ideas of devils, demons, evil spirits, apparitions, ghosts,
ghouls, beasts, chimeras, giants, orcs, magical creatures, all sorts of
supernatural beings _imaginable_.  Essentially, they wanted to incept the mental
constructs of the intangible, specifically those that could be blamed for the
sufferings and malevolence which nature unleashes upon the masses, and their own
obvious doings, shielding them from any repercussions, breaking the causality
cycle at will. Nobody could question the authority if they ascribe something
incomprehensible, unreasonable and exorbitant as culpable. The effects of
natural causes can occlude the consequences of their own actions, concealing the
motive propelling their actions.  Humans are rational enough to delude
themselves, yet also sufficiently rational and intelligent to break out of any
such delusions.  However, under such compulsions, which are coordinated,
enforced, mandated, and coerced, the human minds shall always conform, align and
even begin to propagate the ideas themselves.

This way the persons in power or willing to wield control and hold positions of
power; they could exercise even greater control over the minds of the people and
dictate their behaviors with far greater ease. They could poison the minds,
instill fear of the unknown; whilst pretending to posses the understanding,
knowledge, prowess, authority and the power to nullify these maladies and omens.
_A classic Straw man!_

Now let's fast forward to the modern times. Scientific temperaments arose.
People attained knowledge and higher degrees of freedom in their thoughts, and a
keen awareness about the universe, earth, their immediate as well as remote
surroundings and even the feudal societies themselves. That is, they inculcated
the wisdom which is the culmination of historical learning as well as the
insights which are the analytical anecdotes sourced from past experiences,
accounts and events. They could comprehend what, how and why things are the way
they were. They could reason and validate their reasoning. And they could share
these insights with others.

The grip over their minds loosened because these minds were not feeble anymore,
not wayward and amiable, not easy to persuade or deceive. The average human
began questioning, critical thinking replaced hearsay and folklore. The masses
refused to be swindled, swayed, coalesced, coerced and manipulated. The
compartmentalization of knowledge and the careful hindrances across the social
ladders began faltering. Kings were replaced by presidents and prime ministers.
Nobles by ministers and senators. Knights and warlords by chiefs of armed
defense forces and clandestine organizations. The clergies gave way to modern
religious personas and scientists, researches, psychologists, and doctors.

The cycle was bound to repeat. But this time around, the people desiring power
and authority wanted even greater, even fiercer predicaments, even more
extensive and expansive control, even deeper and involved deceptions and the
most among all of these, a complete dominance of the authority in order to
subvert even opinions.  From oppression to suppression and from misdirection to
the complete illusion of safety and security as well as the terror and concern.
This time the adversaries were _space invaders_. Alien races that are deemed far
too superior and advanced! Why? The narrative reinforces the conclusion by
virtue of its contraposed logic. Nobody could have possible spanned the expanses
of this universe unless they had sufficiently advanced technology, which we are
obviously oblivious to. Alas, with an inherent intent to colonize, terrorize and
dominate our own race, that is. The naivete of humankind in the universal
landscape makes it pliable, therefore vulnerable to discrediting among
inter-galactic civilizations which could in all probabilities exist.
Technologically naive to qualify, to say the least. We, of course perceive
a limit that has been knowingly placed beyond current capabilities, as advanced.

All this seems so lucid and eloquent to place in a syringe and jab into the
collective consciousness and collective knowledge shared by humans. Or to drip
gradually, throttle as required. Attaining an immense degree of control over
narratives and the truth presented to the masses. These ideas and concepts made
the ones in power seem belligerent and possessing noble intentions; sharing the
_truth_ with the commoners, enlightening and illuminating the darkness in
their thoughts and their prejudices with an even stronger, starker yet plausible
and elaborate imagination.

In lieu of such falsehood, military expansion, surveillance, defense budgets,
nuclear deterrence and even weaponizing the outer space can be conceived as an
act of solidarity and strengthening defensive capabilities. And it plays out
perfectly because of the vastness of the universe and its massive mysteries;
that it is impossible to discredit the existence of alien races. And implausible
at the same time for any real evidence to be made pertinent to counter the
claims of those who did paragon this farcical idea that is the impending attack
of an alien race endangering humankind on a global scale. Such theories have
been tactfully postulated keeping in mind and accounting for the ability of the
human brain to perceive and imagine lucid and vivid illusions, its emotion
driven cognition, motivated reasoning and above all the manifestations of
sentiments regarding an unknown intrigue. Again a Straw man!

Humanity in a way is damned! Sovereignty was never meant to be. The inherent
hierarchy of the society stands blatantly gazing at the downtrodden and the
powerless or the naive, staring into their hopeful and gleaming eyes; never to
empower but always to abuse, capitalize and exploit, and obviously to appease
only those who share common profits and motives. Fear is the most potent drug,
no matter the object, and humankind is a gullible subject, subjugated to partial
truths shrouded in abstractions and misleading conundrums. It is almost poetic,
if not fictional, our common history as the human race.
