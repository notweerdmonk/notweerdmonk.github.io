# F(r)actional

If you accomplish at least fifty percent of your goals in a day, the rest fifty
percent remains for the next days. Accomplish fifty percent of the remaining
again the next day, and so on. Every day you fulfill only one halved and let the
other half coast over. But every day you are presented with an opportunity to
accomplish all the goals in a single day.

As days pass, you get closer to completion. One-shot any time, not sooner though.
